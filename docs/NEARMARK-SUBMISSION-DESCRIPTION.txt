NEARMARK is an evidence-first security assessment and payment-enforcement toolkit for autonomous agents on NEAR. It inspects public account and access-key permissions, evaluates payments against owner-defined recipient, method and spending mandates, and includes a restricted native NEAR Gate with expiry and replay controls. Its NASB-0.1 benchmark tests six deterministic security scenarios, while local NEAR Workspaces tests exercise the native Gate. Evidence is classified as confirmed on-chain, Sandbox-observed, code-inferred or unknown. NEAR AI, OutLayer, NEAR Intents and Agent Market integrations are documented with their actual proof and activation limits.
